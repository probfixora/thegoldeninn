About the Property

Welcome to
The Golden Inn

Located in Nijampur Malhaur directly opposite Amity University Road, The Golden Inn is a well-maintained hotel offering comfortable, clean, and thoughtfully presented accommodation. Whether you are visiting for studies, business, or leisure, our team is here to ensure a smooth and welcoming stay.

Guests consistently praise the cooperative staff, spotless rooms, and excellent location. Our on-site restaurant serves vegetarian and Asian breakfast, and we offer free WiFi — rated 10/10 by guests — alongside free private parking.
